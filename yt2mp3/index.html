    // 02matt@benko:/yt2mp3$ cat HOWTO.md

    three steps to your mp3

    1. Watch & queue

      Paste links to build the queue — it plays right on the page and saves in this browser only.

    2. Pick your path

      Instant: hit ⤓ Download → cobalt.tools opens with your link prefilled → audio + Go.
      Local: choose options and hit Copy.

    3. Save the MP3

      Instant saves download straight from cobalt. Local runs transcode with yt-dlp + ffmpeg — the station never touches your files.
